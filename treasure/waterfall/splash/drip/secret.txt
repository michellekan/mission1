Cry Baby heard a rumor that treasure could be found by crying hard enough.
Cry Baby immediately got to work and started crying.
He cried beside a tree and found a wet tree.
He cried beside a rock and found a wet rock.
Encouraged by these results, Cry Baby cried even louder.
A nearby frog gave him a score of nine out of ten for effort.
Cry Baby cried so much that he accidentally watered a patch of grass.
The grass seemed grateful, but it did not become treasure.
He searched every corner while continuing his very professional crying.
A passing squirrel asked if he had tried looking somewhere else.
Cry Baby cried at the squirrel for being too logical.
The squirrel left and found a snack within two minutes.
After hours of crying, searching, and more crying, Cry Baby finally checked the map.
The map clearly showed that the treasure was somewhere else.
Cry Baby stopped crying because there was no treasure here. 😐💎❌
