Cry Baby spent ten minutes looking for his glasses before realizing they were on his face.
The warm afternoon made every task feel twice as difficult.
He opened a book and immediately forgot what page he was reading.
The chair was so comfortable that it felt like a trap.
He yawned loudly enough to scare himself awake.
His coffee looked strong but performed poorly.
The ceiling suddenly became more interesting than his work.
He promised himself he would stay awake for five more minutes.
Five minutes later, he made the same promise again.
The room was quiet except for the sound of his determination fading away.
He tried to focus but his thoughts wandered off without permission.
A passing cloud seemed more productive than he was.
He stared at the clock and somehow time moved backwards.
The blinking cursor appeared to be mocking him.
He considered taking a short break and accidentally planned a vacation.
The book remained open while his attention remained closed.
Every yawn seemed larger than the previous one.
His pillow was not even in the room, yet it felt nearby.
He imagined becoming a professional sleeper.
The idea seemed surprisingly achievable.
Soon he drifted into a strange dream.
In the dream, a squirrel was teaching mathematics.
The squirrel was not a very good teacher.
A sleepy chicken was running a fitness center.
Business was not going well.
A snail offered him directions at an impressive level of slowness.
He followed the instructions and got lost immediately.
After a long journey, he arrived at a cave filled with mystery.
He dreamt that the treasure was not here.
He was right. The treasure was not here.
