CryBaby heard a rumor that the treasure could be found by crying hard enough.
CryBaby immediately got to work and started crying.
He cried beside a tree and found a soaked tree.
He cried beside a rock and found a soaked rock.
Encouraged by these results, CryBaby cried even louder.
A nearby frog gave him a score of nine out of ten for effort.
CryBaby cried so much that he accidentally watered a patch of grass.
The grass seemed grateful, but it did not become a treasure.
He searched every corner while continuing his very professional crying.
After hours of crying, searching, and more crying, CryBaby finally checked the map.
The map clearly showed that the treasure was somewhere else.
CryBaby stopped crying because this is not where the treasure lies. 😐💎❌ 

Happy treasure hunting!
