Labubu met Cry Baby on a dusty trail while searching for treasure.
Cry Baby was crying because he had not found anything all day.
Labubu suggested following the map instead of following tears.
Together they searched under rocks, behind trees, and inside suspicious-looking bushes.
Cry Baby cried a little less and walked a little more.
They eventually found a shiny coin and celebrated too early.
The coin turned out to be a chocolate wrapper.
Labubu laughed so hard that even Cry Baby started laughing.
They checked the map again and realized they were in the wrong place.
The treasure was not here, but at least Cry Baby stopped crying. 😆💎❌
