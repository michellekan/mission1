Crybaby was feeling sleepy.
A strong cup of coffee arrived and achieved absolutely nothing.
He stared at the screen so long that the screen seemed concerned.
The blinking cursor worked harder than he did all afternoon.
He read the same sentence five times and learned something different each time.
A quick stretch somehow turned into a long staring contest with the ceiling.
He checked the clock and was offended by how little time had passed.
The chair became increasingly comfortable and increasingly suspicious.
He tried to focus but his thoughts quietly wandered off without him.
A passing cloud seemed to have more motivation than he did.
He imagined taking a five-minute nap and waking up next Tuesday.
Soon he drifted into a strange dream involving very confident squirrels.
The squirrels were looking for treasure despite having no map.
After hours of searching, one squirrel announced that the treasure was probably elsewhere.
He dreamt that the treasure was not here, and unfortunately he was right.
